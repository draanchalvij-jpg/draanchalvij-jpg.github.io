About

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at IILM University, Greater Noida, with over ten years of academic experience. My research centres on lung cancer detection and classification from CT images, using hybrid deep-learning architectures, attention mechanisms, Transformers and soft computing.

My doctoral thesis, A Novel Methodology of Early Lung Cancer Prediction Using Soft Computing, was submitted at Galgotias University in September 2026. My current direction is explainable, robust and clinically meaningful AI for medical imaging.

Research

My research asks how AI can support earlier, more reliable lung cancer detection from CT images in a way clinicians can trust. I combine local feature extraction, attention and global context modelling, and I focus on making models robust, interpretable and generalizable.

Hybrid deep learning for CT-based lung cancer detection

An EfficientNetV2-S backbone extracts local features, CBAM attention refines them and a Transformer encoder models global dependencies. Multi-class classification on the IQ-OTHNCCD CT dataset, with CLAHE, wavelet denoising, augmentation, SMOTE and focal loss to handle noise and class imbalance.

Papers: IEEE ACET 2025; Journal of Computer Science 2026; IEEE IC2PCT 2026

Soft computing for lung cancer classification

GLCM texture features combined with optimization-assisted feature selection and hybrid stacked machine-learning models. Evaluated with accuracy, precision, recall, F1, AUC and statistical comparison.

Papers: JISEM 2025; IEEE AISC 2023

Explainable and trustworthy healthcare AI

Current direction: interpretable, robust and generalizable models for lung cancer screening and clinical decision support.

    Teaching philosophy

    “From concepts to application, and from learning to innovation.”

    I believe that teaching is not simply about delivering a syllabus; it is about helping students develop the ability to think, question, apply, and innovate.

    Read the full statement

    As an educator in Computer Science and Engineering, I strive to create a learning environment where theoretical concepts are connected with practical implementation and real-world problems. Whether teaching foundational subjects or emerging technologies, my focus is on helping students understand not only what they are learning, but also why it matters and how it can be applied.

    I believe that students learn best when they are actively engaged in the learning process. Therefore, I encourage hands-on learning, problem-solving, experimentation, and discussion. I aim to make complex concepts approachable by connecting them with practical examples, computational tools, and contemporary technological developments.

    My role as a teacher extends beyond the classroom. I see education as an opportunity to mentor students, encourage curiosity, build confidence, and inspire them to explore new ideas. I particularly value the integration of research, innovation, and technology into education, helping students develop the skills required to adapt to an evolving digital world.

    Ultimately, my goal is to help students become not just technically competent professionals, but curious learners, critical thinkers, responsible innovators, and confident problem-solvers.
