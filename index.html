About Me

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at IILM University, with over eight years of academic teaching experience. My academic interests span Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Deep Learning and Medical Image Processing. My doctoral research focuses on the early prediction of lung cancer using soft computing and intelligent computational approaches. My PhD thesis has been submitted at Galgotias University.
